### Part 1: Terms of Service
Last updated: 8 October 2026. Operator: [Company Name], RC [number], [address], Nigeria ("we", "ASSOCIT").

1. What ASSOCIT is
ASSOCIT is a digital record-keeping tool for village unions and associations ("Village Unions"). It lets a Village Union/association record payments for its 6 yearly meetings, keep an audit trail, generate receipts and produce reports.

2. No village money, only platform fees
ASSOCIT does not collect, hold, process, or guarantee any village dues, levies, fines, or contributions. All financial transactions for dues occur offline between members and the village association. ASSOCIT only provides digital record-keeping, receipt generation, and audit trail based on information entered by village admins. The platform owner has no access to village bank accounts and assumes no fiduciary duty over village funds.

In practice: members pay the village bank account or the village treasurer directly. ASSOCIT does not process dues through Paystack or any other payment service and charges no transaction fee or percentage on any village payment.

ASSOCIT DOES charge: (a) Yearly platform subscription fee of ₦30,000 Basic / ₦50,000 Premium paid by Village Union to use ASSOCIT for all its meetings per year, and (b) Sponsor billboard advertising fees paid by sponsors. Both are paid to [Company Name:_______________________________________________] outside the platform and are licence/advertising fees, not village funds. Onboarding is FREE.

3. What ASSOCIT is not
This platform is not a financial institution, is not licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN), and is not a cooperative society. It does not take deposits, give loans, run savings schemes or offer insurance. Because no village funds are held by ASSOCIT, no CBN regulation on the collection of funds applies to our service.

4. Accounts and who is responsible for entries
- Each Village Union appoints its admins.
- A record is only as accurate as what a village admin or member enters.
- A village admin who enters a false record is personally answerable to the Village Union. The audit trail stores the admin's name, action and time.
- Verification of a member's proof of payment is a decision of the village admin, not of ASSOCIT.

5. Sponsor billboards and advertising
The platform shows up to three sponsor cards. Sponsor fees are paid to the platform owner and are part of our income. We are not liable for what a sponsor sells or does.

6. Acceptable use
Do not upload false proofs, break into another village's records, or use ASSOCIT to collect money from the public.

7. Your data
Each Village Union's records belong to that Village Union. We process them on its instructions.

8. Availability and liability
We work to keep ASSOCIT running but do not promise it will be always online. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for lost dues, disputes, or wrong entries by admins.

9. Changes, ending and law
We may update these terms. A Village Union may stop using ASSOCIT at any time and may export its records first. Governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Courts of [Lagos State] have jurisdiction.
