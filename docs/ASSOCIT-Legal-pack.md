### Part 1: Terms of Service
Last updated: 8 October 2026. Operator: [Company Name], RC [number], [address], Nigeria ("we", "ASSOCIT"). Data Protection Officer: [DPO Name, email, phone] - Registered with NDPC.

1. What ASSOCIT is
ASSOCIT is a digital record-keeping tool for village unions and associations ("Village Unions"). It lets a Village Union/association record payments for its 6 yearly meetings, keep an audit trail, generate receipts and produce reports.

2. No village money, only platform fees
ASSOCIT does not collect, hold, process, or guarantee any village dues, levies, fines, or contributions. All financial transactions for dues occur offline between members and the village association. ASSOCIT only provides digital record-keeping, receipt generation, and audit trail based on information entered by village admins. The platform owner has no access to village bank accounts and assumes no fiduciary duty over village funds.

In practice: members pay the village bank account or the village treasurer directly. ASSOCIT does not process dues through Paystack or any other payment service and charges no transaction fee or percentage on any village payment.

ASSOCIT DOES charge: (a) Yearly platform subscription fee of ₦30,000 Basic / ₦50,000 Premium paid by Village Union to use ASSOCIT for all its meetings per year, and (b) Sponsor billboard advertising fees paid by sponsors. Both are paid to [Company Name:_______________________________________________] outside the platform via bank transfer to [Bank Name, Account Number, Account Name] and are licence/advertising fees, not village funds. Onboarding is FREE.

3. What ASSOCIT is not
This platform is not a financial institution, is not licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN), and is not a cooperative society. It does not take deposits, give loans, run savings schemes or offer insurance. The parties acknowledge that Licensor does not hold, transmit, or have custody of Client funds and therefore does not carry on banking business within the meaning of the Banks and Other Financial Institutions Act (BOFIA) 2020. Nothing herein shall be construed as banking business.

4. Accounts and who is responsible for entries
- Each Village Union appoints its admins.
- A record is only as accurate as what a village admin or member enters.
- A village admin who enters a false record is personally answerable to the Village Union. The audit trail stores the admin's name, action and time.
- Verification of a member's proof of payment is a decision of the village admin, not of ASSOCIT.
- Platform Owner emergency access (break-glass) is logged in separate platform_admin_logs with 60-minute expiry, is subject to audit, and is disclosed to affected Village Unions as "Platform maintenance" without revealing Owner identity to preserve neutrality.

**4A. Optional Device-Native Biometric Login (Bank Pattern)**
ASSOCIT may offer optional device-native login using WebAuthn passkeys. If enabled by the user, fingerprint or face verification is performed locally on the user's own device by the device operating system. Biometric templates, images, and raw biometric data never leave the user's device, are never transmitted to ASSOCIT, and are never stored on Licensor's servers. ASSOCIT only receives a cryptographic yes/no assertion and stores a public key and credential identifier to validate future assertions. Users may enable or disable this feature at any time and may always use phone and one-time password login as an alternative. Staff multi-factor authentication requirements remain mandatory where applicable.

5. Sponsor billboards and advertising
The platform shows up to three sponsor cards per village dashboard. Sponsor fees are paid to the platform owner and are part of our income. We are not liable for what a sponsor sells or does. Sponsor content must comply with APCON regulations and must not be deceptive.

6. Acceptable use
Do not upload false proofs, break into another village's records, or use ASSOCIT to collect money from the public. Any breach may result in suspension after 7 days notice and opportunity to remedy, save for fraud which may result in immediate suspension.

7. Your data
Each Village Union's records belong to that Village Union. We process them on its instructions under a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) attached as Schedule 1, per NDPA 2023.

8. Availability and liability
We work to keep ASSOCIT running but do not promise it will be always online. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for lost dues, disputes, or wrong entries by admins. Total liability under this Agreement is limited to subscription fees paid in 12 months before claim, except for willful misconduct, gross negligence, or breach of data protection obligations where liability shall be as provided by law.

9. Changes, ending and law
We may update these terms with 30 days notice. A Village Union may stop using ASSOCIT at any time and may export its records first. Governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Seat of arbitration: Lagos, Nigeria. Courts of Lagos State have jurisdiction.

### Part 2: Privacy Policy (NDPA 2023)
Last updated: 8 October 2026. Contact: [name, email]. DPO: [DPO Name, email, phone].

1. Who is responsible
Each Village Union is the data controller for its member records. [Company Name:______________________________________________] is the data processor under Schedule 1 DPA. For sponsor accounts and subscription billing, we are the controller.

2. The data we handle
Members: Name, phone, village, ward, member ID, payment record, receipt image. Admins: Name, log. Village Union: Union name, RC, contact, subscription payment reference. Sponsors: Shop name, logo, WhatsApp. We do not collect bank account numbers, BVN, NIN, card numbers, or any biometric templates.

**2A. Biometric Login Data**
We do not collect, process, or store biometric templates, fingerprint images, facial images, or voice prints. Where a user opts to use device-native biometric login via WebAuthn, the biometric verification occurs solely on the user's device. What ASSOCIT stores is limited to a non-biometric public key, credential identifier, and counter for passkey validation, which cannot be used to reconstruct any biometric data. This does not constitute processing of Sensitive Personal Data by Licensor under NDPA 2023.

3. Legal basis and consent
Members tick consent box. Village legitimate interest also applies for record-keeping per its constitution. For optional device-native biometric login, the lawful basis is the user's explicit consent, which may be withdrawn at any time by disabling the feature in account settings or by contacting the Village Union admin, without affecting lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

3A. Cross-border transfer
Data is hosted on Supabase / AWS infrastructure which may be located outside Nigeria (EU/US). Transfer is based on NDPA s.43 - consent, contract necessity, and adequacy via Standard Contractual Clauses with our sub-processors. List of sub-processors available on request.

4. Who sees the data
Member sees own, admins see own village only. We do not sell data. Sub-processors: Supabase Inc (hosting), [SMS provider] (OTP delivery) - bound by DPA.

5. How long we keep it
Payment records 6 years per CAMA 2020 s.378 (accounting records) and per Village Union constitution audit requirement. Sponsor/subscription records 6 years per tax law. Passkey public keys are retained until the user deletes them or the account is deactivated, then deleted within 30 days. Inactive accounts anonymized after 6 years.

6. Your rights
Under NDPA 2023 you may ask to see, correct, delete, object, portability, and withdraw consent. We will respond within one month as required by NDPA. Procedure: Ask village admin first, or write to DPO at [email]. You have right to lodge complaint with Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) - ndpc.gov.ng.

7. Security and breaches
Village records separated by Row Level Security (RLS) per association. Encryption at rest and in transit. If breach likely to risk rights, we tell affected Village Union without delay and notify NDPC within 72 hours of becoming aware, and notify affected data subjects without undue delay where high risk, describing nature, likely consequences, and mitigation.

8. Children
For adult members only (18+). We do not knowingly process children's data.

9. Changes
We will post changes here with 30 days notice for material changes.
