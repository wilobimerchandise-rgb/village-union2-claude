Removed from every document: transaction fees, Paystack for dues, any percentage cut, and any holding or disbursing of village funds.

Onboarding for villages is FREE. Villages only pay yearly subscription to use platform.

Before you use these with real clients, a Nigerian lawyer should check:
• The no-CBN-regulation statement. It is your position, not a ruling. A lawyer must confirm that recording payments, issuing receipts, charging subscription, and showing sponsor ads stays outside CBN, SEC and cooperative-society rules.
• The 6-year retention period. You asked for it; NDPA 2023 does not set a fixed number, so each village must be told it is its own policy.
• The exact CAMA 2020 references for incorporated trustees, which differ by how each village is registered.
• Your company name, RC number, address, governing court and the data protection officer details. They are in square brackets.
• Whether you need NDPC registration as a data controller or processor of major importance.
These are drafts, not legal advice.

### Part 1: Terms of Service
Last updated: 8 October 2026. Operator: [Company Name], RC [number], [address], Nigeria ("we", "ASSOCIT").

1. What ASSOCIT is
ASSOCIT is a digital record-keeping tool for village unions and associations ("Village Unions"). It lets a Village Union record payments, keep an audit trail, generate receipts and produce reports. It works like a spreadsheet with a receipt printer. Nothing more.

2. No village money, only platform fees
ASSOCIT does not collect, hold, process, or guarantee any village dues, levies, fines, or contributions. All financial transactions for dues occur offline between members and the village association. ASSOCIT only provides digital record-keeping, receipt generation, and audit trail based on information entered by village admins. The platform owner has no access to village bank accounts and assumes no fiduciary duty over village funds.

In practice: members pay the village bank account or the village treasurer directly. ASSOCIT does not process dues through Paystack or any other payment service and charges no transaction fee or percentage on any village payment.

ASSOCIT DOES charge: (a) Yearly platform subscription fee paid by Village Union to use ASSOCIT, and (b) Sponsor billboard advertising fees paid by sponsors. Both are paid to [Company Name] outside the platform and are licence/advertising fees, not village funds.

Onboarding is FREE. No onboarding fee is charged.

3. What ASSOCIT is not
This platform is not a financial institution, is not licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN), and is not a cooperative society. It does not take deposits, give loans, run savings schemes or offer insurance. The "life insurance levy" and "burial levy" are the Village Union's own contributions, not insurance products sold by us.
Because no village funds are held by ASSOCIT, no CBN regulation on the collection of funds applies to our service. Each Village Union stays responsible for its own compliance, including under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 (CAMA 2020) where it is registered as incorporated trustees.

4. Accounts and who is responsible for entries
• Each Village Union appoints its admins. Admins and members must keep their login details private.
• A record is only as accurate as what a village admin or member enters. ASSOCIT does not check it against any bank.
• A village admin who enters a false, altered or misleading record is personally answerable to the Village Union and, where the law allows, to affected members. The audit trail stores the admin's name, the action and the time, and is evidence of who entered it.
• Verification of a member's proof of payment is a decision of the village admin, not of ASSOCIT. A receipt from ASSOCIT confirms that an admin marked a payment as verified. It is not a bank confirmation.

5. Sponsor billboards and advertising
The platform shows up to three sponsor cards on member dashboards. Some are Lagos-wide, some are for one village. Sponsor fees are paid to the platform owner and are part of our income. They are advertising income, not village funds.
• Sponsors are checked before they go live, but we do not guarantee any sponsor's goods, services, prices or claims. We are not liable for what a sponsor sells or does.
• A sponsor card does not mean the Village Union or its leaders endorse that sponsor.
• A member who chats with a sponsor on WhatsApp deals with the sponsor directly, at their own risk.
• Report a sponsor at [support email]. We may pause or remove any sponsor at any time.

6. Acceptable use
Do not upload false proofs of payment, break into another village's records, copy another village's data, or use ASSOCIT to collect money from the public. We may suspend an account or village that does.

7. Your data
Our Privacy Policy explains what personal data we handle and why. Each Village Union's records belong to that Village Union. We process them on its instructions.

8. Availability and liability
We work to keep ASSOCIT running but do not promise it will be error-free or always online. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for lost dues, unpaid levies, disputes between members and their Village Union, wrong entries by admins, or losses caused by a sponsor. Nothing here limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

### Part 2: Privacy Policy (NDPA 2023)
Last updated: 8 October 2026. This policy follows the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA). Data protection contact: [name, email].

1. Who is responsible
Each Village Union decides what records it keeps about its members and why. It is the data controller for those records. [Company Name] (ASSOCIT) is the data processor: we store and display the records on the Village Union's instructions. For sponsor accounts, subscription billing, and website visitors, we are the controller.

2. The data we handle
We keep personal data to a minimum.
- Members: Name, phone number, village, ward, member ID - To run member list
- Members: Payment record: levy, amount, date, method, bank or teller reference - To keep ledger and issue receipts
- Members: Receipt image or proof of payment - So admin can verify
- Admins: Name, admin ID, timestamped log of each action - To show who entered record
- Village Union: Union name, RC, contact person, subscription payment reference - To manage subscription
- Sponsors: Shop name, logo, WhatsApp number, sponsorship dates and payment reference - To show sponsor card

We do not collect bank account numbers, BVN, NIN, card numbers or PINs. Please do not upload a receipt that shows your full account number. Cover it first. Subscription and sponsor payments are handled offline via bank transfer to [Company Name]. If Paystack is used for sponsor cards, Paystack keeps its own records.

3. Legal basis and consent
Members are asked to tick a box that says they agree to their records being held on ASSOCIT before an account is used. The Village Union's legitimate interest in keeping accurate association records also applies. You may withdraw consent by telling your village admin. Records the Village Union must keep may still be held.

4. Who sees the data
• A member sees their own records and the village summary. Names in the village ledger are shown only if the Village Union turns that on.
• A village's admins see that village only. Other villages cannot see it.
• Our staff do not look at village records, except to give support when asked. Any such access is logged.
• We do not sell personal data. Sponsor cards show a shop's own details, never member data.

5. How long we keep it
Payment and financial records are kept for 6 years, because associations are expected to keep their accounts for that period. This is the Village Union's responsibility as controller, and it may ask us to delete earlier where the law allows. Proof images may be deleted sooner on the Village Union's request. Sponsor and subscription records are kept for 6 years for our own accounts.

6. Your rights
Under the NDPA you may ask to see, correct, or in some cases delete your data, object to its use, and ask for a copy. Ask your village admin first. You may also write to us, or complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

7. Security and breaches
Village records are separated by village. Passwords are stored hashed. Admin actions are logged. If we learn of a breach likely to risk people's rights, we will tell the affected Village Union without delay and notify the NDPC within 72 hours, as the NDPA requires. The Village Union must tell affected members.

8. Children
The service is for adult members. We do not knowingly keep data about children as account holders.

9. Changes
We will post changes here with a new date.
