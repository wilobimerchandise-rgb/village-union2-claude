Questions unions ask first.

Can one village see another village's records?

No. Each village has its own members, admins and ledger. A signed-in user can only open the village they signed in to.

Who can change a record?

Only admins. Members can upload proof and view records. When an admin verifies or edits anything, their name and the time are saved on the record and in the activity log.

What if a member uploads the wrong proof?

The admin rejects it with a reason, for example "amount does not match". The member sees the reason and uploads again. Rejected payments never count in a report.

How do the PDF reports work?

Open Reports, choose a month or a year and press Download PDF. Every completed month and year is listed so you can download earlier reports at any time.

Does it work on a phone with a weak signal?

Yes. The pages are light, and proof photos are shrunk on the phone before upload so they send quickly.
